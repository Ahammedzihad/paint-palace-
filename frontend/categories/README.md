# Frontend Categories

Category definitions and visual showcases for the 6 core Paint Palace paint divisions:

1. **Interior Paint**: High-end living room finishes and ceiling paints.
2. **Exterior Paint**: Weather-resistant villa and building exterior coatings.
3. **Wood & Metal Paint**: Furniture, cabinetry, doors, and architectural metal protection.
4. **Primers & Putty**: Surface leveling, crack-filling, and undercoats.
5. **Waterproofing**: Roof, terrace, and foundation moisture barriers.
6. **Auto & Specialty Paint**: Automotive refinishing, PU coatings, and metallic finishes.
